# SEO, reviews and outreach owner actions

## Review workflow

1. Ask only customers whose orders are confirmed or fulfilled.
2. Send one neutral request asking for an honest rating and written experience; never request a specific score.
3. Moderate for privacy, abuse, unrelated content and unverifiable claims. Do not edit sentiment or create reviews.
4. Publish reviewer name or initials only with permission. Keep email addresses private.
5. Mark verified purchase only when the review can be matched to an order.

Anti-fraud checks: duplicate device/contact patterns, repeated wording, unusual bursts, conflicts of interest and incentives that were not disclosed. Reject fabricated, purchased or self-authored reviews.

## Indian reviewer categories

- Independent fragrance and attar reviewers
- Indian men’s and women’s grooming creators
- Punjab, Ludhiana and Chandigarh lifestyle creators
- Wedding, gifting and festive-shopping publishers
- Oud, musk and perfume-oil enthusiast communities

## Outreach template

> Hello [Name], I’m contacting you for Rehmat Panjab, a Punjab-based concentrated perfume-oil brand. We appreciate your work on [specific relevant topic]. Would you be interested in considering a complimentary product sample for an independent review? There is no requirement for positive coverage. Please share your usual terms, audience information, disclosure approach and usage-rights options. No commitment is made until we confirm the exact product, deliverables and budget in writing.

## Sample-campaign checklist

- Confirm creator identity, audience location and engagement quality.
- Agree exact product, quantity, deliverables, dates, cost and payment terms.
- Require clear `Ad`, `Sponsored` or `Gifted` disclosure appropriate to the relationship and platform.
- Confirm whether the brand may repost, edit, advertise or archive the content, and for how long.
- Record shipment, publication URL, reach, engagement, referral traffic, sales and learnings.
- Never script a positive opinion or suppress honest criticism.

## Creator record fields

Creator name · platform/profile · audience size · primary audience location · contact · fragrance category · proposed deliverables · dates · sample cost · paid fee · disclosure · usage rights · approval status · tracking URL/code · reach · engagement · conversions · notes.

## Search and Merchant Center actions

- Verify `www.rehmatpanjab.com` in Google Search Console and submit `/sitemap.xml`.
- Create Merchant Center account-level India shipping rules: free at eligible ₹1,000 product subtotal; the available delivery charge below that must be configured before feed activation.
- Create and assign the return-policy label `Rehmat Panjab India returns`, then review the generated `/google-merchant-feed.xml` before submission.
- Do not activate Merchant Center, paid advertising or outreach spending without the owner approving the account, recipient and budget.
